What We Do

About ROBOSPHERE

Pioneering mechanical design, embedded software, and artificial intelligence

🤖

Autonomous Systems

Designing mobile robots and drones capable of spatial navigation, obstacle avoidance, and decision-making without human control.

🧠

Mechatronics & AI

Integrating neural networks with precise kinematic hardware to enable vision-based manipulation and deep learning motor controls.

🛸

Aerial Robotics

Engineering customized quadcopters and UAVs equipped with computer vision for mapping, inspection, and autonomous delivery.

⚡

Hardware Prototyping

From custom PCB design to 3D printing and CNC machining, turning high-tech concepts into functional physical machines.
